# Maintenance

Workshop observations are not manufacturer limits unless explicitly identified below.

## Bottom bracket (WAR-001)

After removal, clean the housing, axle, cups, seals, and chain-cover ring with cleaner, a lint-free cloth, and a brush. Use compressed air cautiously and never spin a bearing at high speed with air. Remove stale grease, let parts dry, and apply fresh bearing grease before installation.

The documented Bafang unit ran smoothly, retained adequate lubrication, and showed no unusual corrosion, so it was considered reusable after service. Slight sensor-body play appeared normal. Replace its cracked right seal; the clean left seal appeared reusable. Inspect the six-pole Higo lead and keep the frame drain opening clear. See the [removal procedure](../disassembly/drivetrain-and-bottom-bracket.md#bafang-bottom-bracket).

## Chain and chain tensioner (WAR-002)

Brush and wipe the chain with a compatible chain cleaner, dry it completely, then apply bicycle-chain lubricant evenly and wipe off the excess. A vice-and-ruler guide was used in the workshop to draw the loose chain through a cloth, but avoid clamping or bending links. Preserve the master link's recorded running direction.

Disassemble the tensioner only in a contained work area because it is spring-loaded. Clean the wheels, `689 RS` bearings, washers, spring, and housing individually. Confirm that bearings turn smoothly, apply only suitable lubricant where required, and keep lubricant away from brakes. Do not return cracked or broken tensioner parts to service.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The chain cleaner used in the workshop attacked a plastic storage container. Check chemical compatibility, ventilation, gloves, disposal instructions, and every polymer part before soaking it.
{% endhint %}

## Corrosion and water management

Clean and assess the kickstand area, lower fork, crank fasteners, tensioner/Shifter area, and brake hardware where corrosion was observed. Use a material-compatible corrosion protectant only after determining whether the damage is superficial. Never contaminate pads or rotors. The bottom-bracket shell drain is intentional and must remain unobstructed.

## Inspection schedule (manufacturer information recorded in the logs)

* **Monthly:** inspect both wheels, handlebar, pedals, saddle, brake pads, and chain; lubricate the chain, clean the frame, and check for applicable firmware updates. Maintain tyre pressure at 40–65 psi.
* **Annually:** service by an authorised VanMoof service partner.
* **Mileage guidance:** chain and sprocket at 6,000–8,000 km; rear hub at 10,000 km.

Intervals do not override inspection after damage, water ingress, abnormal noise, looseness, braking changes, or a crash.

## Kickstand corrosion

Slight corrosion was observed around the removed kickstand. Clean and inspect the precise area and its fastener, then use a compatible light corrosion-protection product. The documented main fastener is an estimated M10 × 30 mm bolt with 8 mm hex and a 10 × 16 × 2.8 mm spring washer; its torque remains unknown.
