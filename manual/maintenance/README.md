# Maintenance

Maintenance procedures cover cleaning, lubrication, inspection, and preservation. Planned workshop records include:

* `WAR-001`: clean and inspect the bottom bracket
* `WAR-002`: clean, lubricate, and preserve the chain

Each procedure should specify service conditions or intervals where known, required materials, inspection criteria, wear limits, environmental precautions, and a post-service check. Do not invent an interval or wear limit where no reliable source or workshop measurement exists.

## Kickstand corrosion

Slight or incipient corrosion was observed in the kickstand area during one repair. At the next service, clean and inspect the affected area and apply a suitable light lubricant or corrosion-protection product. The precise affected location and a compatible product have not yet been established; verify both before treatment and avoid contaminating braking surfaces.

Also inspect the kickstand fastener condition before reassembly. Its [recorded drive and tool](../reference/fasteners.md#fastener-register) are known, but its tightening torque remains unspecified.
