## Torque not yet specified

No manufacturer torque has yet been recorded for the following assemblies:

* battery cover
* bottom bracket
* cranks
* chain tensioner
* fenders
* Smart Cartridge
* front light
* antenna cover
* kickstand
* brake calipers
* luggage rack

Do not infer a tightening torque from the fastener size alone. When adding a value, always include units and distinguish a VanMoof specification from a component-manufacturer specification, a workshop-verified value, or an unverified estimate.
