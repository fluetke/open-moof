# Disassembly

Disassembly documents follow the chronological order in which parts are removed from the bicycle. Each procedure should identify prerequisites, tools, fasteners, electrical or hydraulic connections, hazards, and the storage or labelling needed for removed parts.

The current [full-disassembly record](../../repair-guides/full-disassembly.md) will become the top-level sequence. Existing component procedures provide detailed steps for:

* [removing the front wheel and hub motor](../../repair-guides/hub-motor/removing-the-front-wheel-and-hub-motor.md)
* [opening the hub motor](../../repair-guides/hub-motor/opening-the-motor.md)
* [removing the cable harness](../../repair-guides/cable-harness/README.md)

New chronological procedures should use `DEM-###` identifiers and link to the applicable fastener, tool, connector, and torque references.

## Battery removal workshop findings

During one documented S3 repair, the rear tyre was deflated before the battery was successfully removed. The exposed interior appeared clean after removal. This observation comes from repair audio notes and is **not yet a complete removal procedure**: the reason for deflating the tyre, the required pressure, omitted prerequisite steps, and the battery's removal direction remain to be verified.

## Kickstand removal workshop findings

The kickstand was removed with an [8 mm hex key](../reference/tools.md#tool-register). Slight or incipient corrosion was found in the kickstand area. The precise location, condition and number of its fasteners, and the required torque were not captured. See the [maintenance guidance](../maintenance/README.md#kickstand-corrosion) before reassembly.

## Front hub motor inspection finding

After loosening the front axle nuts, the hub motor was checked for free rotation. On the documented bicycle it continued to rotate freely and felt subjectively as good as usual. Record the order of the axle nuts and washers during removal because their exact arrangement has not yet been documented. See the [assembly warning](../assembly/README.md#front-hub-motor-axle) before installing the wheel.
