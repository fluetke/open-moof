# Reverse engineering

Reverse-engineering records describe construction, materials, dimensions, interfaces, known weaknesses, and community-developed replacements. Initial subjects are:

* `RE-001`: chain guard
* `RE-002`: bottom bracket
* `RE-003`: cable harness

Keep measured facts separate from assumptions. Record measurement method, tolerances, sample bike or component revision, and photographs. Link resulting CAD or printable models from the [appendix](../appendix/README.md).
