# Reverse engineering

All material identifications on this page are visual workshop hypotheses until tested.

## Chain tensioner

The moulded housing concentrates mounting and closure load at a small eye; two eyes were broken and multiple microcracks were visible. A replacement design must preserve chain line, spring preload and stops, bearing fits, guard clearance, and fastener stack. Capture dimensions, photographs, fracture surfaces, spring rate, loads, and the full `689 RS` bearing marking before modelling. Do not copy the weak eye without structural validation.

## Chain cover

The 38-tooth chainring's cover is clipped on after crank removal. Its lower arm and several retaining tabs were broken and the plastic had aged. It may be glass-filled polyamide, but this is unverified. Its size may require a modular printable design; determine material, measurements, joining method, heat/chemical resistance, and chain clearance first. An official replacement was listed in the source notes for €25, but price and availability are time-sensitive.

## Bottom bracket and frame

The Bafang assembly uses a left keyed support whose tabs prevent rotation and a right left-threaded protective cup. The cracked right and good left removable seals may be standard Bafang items; confirm profiles and dimensions before cross-referencing. The bottom-bracket shell includes a deliberate water drain.

## Handlebar and controls

The bar appeared to combine a machined aluminium casting with a bent welded tube, but neither material nor process is confirmed. Replacement grips must reproduce the button-wire groove and leave room for the cable, silicone-mounted button, retaining set screw, lever clamp, and steerer clearance. Record spacer count and cable route before developing replacements.

## Other identification records

* Crank: forged, 170 mm, `GJ-9AS`; five-bolt, replaceable 38-tooth ring.
* Pedals: right `CM-R0H2`; left marking uncertain.
* Brake calipers: front `J19 8380`; rear `J19 7901`.
* Kick Lock bolt: visually stainless with an unidentified black centre insert.
